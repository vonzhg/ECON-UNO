Course description

This course introduces the measurement and behavior of the aggregate economy: GDP, unemployment, inflation, long-run growth, and the roles of fiscal and monetary policy. We also cover the basic tools of opportunity cost, gains from trade, and supply and demand that every later economics course uses.

Learning objectives

  • Read the main macroeconomic statistics and know what they do and do not measure.
  • Use supply and demand, and later AD–AS, to analyze shocks and policy.
  • Explain growth, unemployment, and inflation in plain language and with simple models.
  • Evaluate fiscal and monetary policy with the tools of the course, not slogans.
